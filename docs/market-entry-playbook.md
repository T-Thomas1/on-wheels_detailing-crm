# Market-Entry Playbook (new service area)

How On-Wheels opens a new market without spreading thin. The rule is one
market at a time: confirm traction before opening the next.

## Sequence (per new market)

1. Pick ONE market (e.g. Houston TX). Do not launch two at once.
2. GBP — create the Google Business Profile for that area. Storefront listing
   if there is a fixed location, otherwise a service-area listing.
3. City landing page — add a geo page on the site with unique content + local
   schema (AutoDetailing/LocalBusiness). Never duplicate a page into a doorway
   page; each city page is written fresh.
4. Geo Meta ad — run a geo-targeted Facebook/Instagram ad at the new area to
   put the business in front of local owners.
5. Seed first reviews — get the first ~5 customers to leave reviews, and ask
   them to name the town ("mobile detailing in Marysville"). Reviews that name
   the town are the cheapest local-SEO win.

## Rules

- One market at a time; measure before moving on.
- Unique content per city page, no doorway/duplicate pages.
- Reviews that name the town compound into local ranking (trust moat).
