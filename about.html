Our Journey

From passion to profession - our story of growth and dedication

The Beginning

In 2020, what started as a search for purpose became the foundation of On-Wheels Detailing. It wasn't about cleaning cars - it was about the transformation, the meticulous process, and the incredible results that brought vehicles back to life.

Our Philosophy

We believe that true excellence comes from respecting the process. Every vehicle we detail receives the same level of care and attention we'd give our own. It's not just a service; it's a craft we've honed through dedication and love for what we do.

The Personal Touch

For me, this is more than a business - it's a craft. That's why I'm personally involved in every detail of your vehicle's service, collaborating with my skilled team to deliver stunning results. This hands-on approach allows me to get to know you and your car, ensuring we meet your exact vision every time. You'll always be working directly with the owner.

The Transformation

Over the past five years, we've grown from a simple idea fueled by passion to a trusted service in our community. Each detail tells a story - of revival, of care, and of the passion that drives us forward.
